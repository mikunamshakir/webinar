About this webinar

Generative AI is reshaping how software gets built, how content gets created, and how people work day to day — but for many, it's still a black box. This free, one-hour session breaks down what generative AI actually is, how it works under the hood, and where it's already showing up in everyday tools.

No prior AI or machine learning background is required. Whether you're a student, a developer, or just curious about the technology behind tools like ChatGPT and image generators, this session is built to make the concepts clear and practical.

What you'll learn

  • What generative AI is, and how it's different from traditional software
  • The basics of how large language models are trained and generate output
  • Real-world applications across writing, code, and image generation
  • Where the technology is heading, and how to start exploring it yourself

Format

A live, one-hour session on Google Meet with a short presentation followed by open Q&A — bring your questions.

Who it's for

Anyone curious about AI: students, developers, educators, and professionals looking to understand generative AI before diving deeper into it.
